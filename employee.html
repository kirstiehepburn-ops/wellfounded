Your sense of meaning at work has dipped this year. The following recommendations are drawn directly from Paul Dolan's research. Mark each one as done when you try it - we'll ask how it went.

Map your impact

Take 20 minutes this week to write down three ways your work directly helps a real person - a colleague, a client, or an end user. Paul's research shows that making impact concrete and specific - not abstract - is what shifts the feeling of significance.

Have a "purpose conversation" with your manager

Ask your manager to help you connect your current projects to team or org goals. Studies show that employees who can articulate the link between their tasks and broader outcomes score 18 points higher on Significance within one quarter.

Protect one focus block per day

Your Pleasure score is being held back by fragmented attention - one of the most common day-to-day wellbeing killers in office environments. Block 90 minutes daily with no Slack, no email, no meetings. Even two weeks of this significantly improves your experience of work.

Pleasure Daily habit

Invest in one meaningful work relationship

Togetherness scores are strongly predicted by the depth of a few key relationships rather than the breadth of many. Identify one colleague you'd like to know better and make a small, deliberate effort - a coffee, a shared project, a genuine question.
