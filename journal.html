PAST Journal · Daily practice
Five minutes.
Four questions.
A better tomorrow.
Based on Professor Paul Dolan's research, noticing moments of Pleasure, Achievement, Significance and Togetherness each day builds your awareness of what actually makes work feel good - and helps you do more of it.
P · Pleasure
What felt good?
"What moment did you actually enjoy today?"
A · Achievement
What moved?
"What did you make real progress on?"
S · Significance
What mattered?
"What felt meaningful - even briefly?"
T · Togetherness
Who mattered?
"Which connection felt real today?"
~5 minutes · best done on the commute home
